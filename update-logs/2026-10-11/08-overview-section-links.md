# Overview section links

## What changed

Made Learning activities, Current month, and My learning overview cards
keyboard-accessible navigation controls. Learning activities scrolls to the
Upcoming activities section; Current month scrolls to the month currently shown
in Learning Calendar; My learning continues to open its registration view.

## Why

The overview counters now provide direct routes to the information each count
summarises.

## Alternative considered

Using a separate text link inside each card would reduce the hit target and
make the counters less obviously actionable.

## Follow-ups

Preserve these target IDs if the page sections are extracted later.
