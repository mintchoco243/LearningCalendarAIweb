# 12 — Refined skill-path hero

**What:** Replaced the generic hero wireframe with a three-node skill path: Start, Build, and Master. The Build node is the focused state, with a single red route and restrained hover response.

**Why:** The approved hero direction needs a gaming-inspired visual that gives the page an identity without repeating the calendar content or overwhelming the orientation copy.

**Alternative considered:** A denser level-select landscape with multiple HUD counters and routes was explored, then rejected because it competed with the hero headline and became visually noisy.

**Follow-ups:** Confirm the visual balance in the browser at the 1920 × 900 desktop baseline before extending the motif elsewhere.
