# L&D Activities Calendar Prototype

## Plan

1. Establish governance, documentation, source, Storybook, and update-log
   structure. Verify that indexes map the created paths.
2. Configure React, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Storybook. Verify that the app and
   Storybook build commands resolve.
3. Create mock activities across a year with summary and detailed training
   fields. Verify that upcoming, past, and varied month states exist.
4. Build a yearly calendar, selected-day activity list, and upcoming activity
   summary. Verify that selecting a month or day changes the visible activities.
5. Build the activity detail panel with training and trainer information.
   Verify that opening and closing details preserves the selected activity.
6. Add local sign-up and cancellation state plus a registrations view. Verify
   that all registration status indicators update in the same session.
7. Add component stories and perform local build verification.
